How to play

Gather the Krystals

  1. The Hub. Pick your Krystalker and up to 3 gems at the Round Table, read the Archive, train on dummies, and set out through the Expedition Gate.
  2. Biomes. Each biome is a group of rooms with its own monsters and a broken altar (◇). Restore it (F) to start the biome's challenge. The altar shows the recommended level, with ☠ in red when you are below it.
  3. Challenges. The biome seals: nobody inside can leave, but allies can still walk in to help. Monsters keep pouring in and the biome's boss rises in one of its rooms. Inside and outside cannot see each other. Beat the boss and the altar becomes a Krystal Shrine for levelling. If everyone inside falls, the challenge fails. The Wildfire burns seals open.
  4. Boss chests. Every boss drops a chest. Each player opens it once and picks 1 of 3 relics. Bosses with a higher recommended level give better odds. Unopened chests stay marked on your map.
  5. Gold flies straight to your purse and is shared by the whole team. Rooms slowly refill with monsters to farm. Two linked teleporter rooms (◎) connect far corners of the map.
  6. The Wildfire ignites twice per Act and closes on a Gatekeeper. Act II is longer and has stronger monsters. After Gatekeeper II the fire stays and a portal opens to The Bazaar. Its northern portal leads to the Dungeon Master's own arena.
  7. The Labyrinth. Strong monsters that pay well. Its altar wakes three tower guardians (Lv 10). Beat all three and the Keep's master rises (Lv 13). Clearing it is the surest way to max level (15). Plan for it in Act II with a full team.
  8. Dead? Press 1, 2 or 3 to watch a teammate. If they are sealed in a challenge, you only see what is right around them.
  9. Recommended level. Bosses and Labyrinth monsters have a recommended level (Gatekeeper I: 5, Gatekeeper II: 10, Dungeon Master: 15). Every level you are below it, you deal 8% less damage and take 12% more. Cleared rooms slowly refill with monsters to farm.

Elements and weaknesses: Fire, Frost, Bleed, Poison, Shock, Shadow. Bosses list their weakness: matching hits deal +50%, matching damage over time deals double. A frozen boss is freeze-immune for 12s.

Dying while exploring is not revivable: you respawn after a few seconds, drop one level, and leave that level's gold where you fell.

Death. Only during a Gatekeeper or Dungeon Master fight can a fallen ally be revived. Anywhere else, falling means death: you respawn at a shrine one level lower and leave your gold where you fell.

Flasks. You start with 2 flasks (R): each heals 20% of your health. Standing at a Krystal Shrine with no enemies near refills your flasks and health. Six small rooms hold Krystal Fonts (⚱): your map shows where they are, but not how to get there. Channel one for 5 seconds (taking damage breaks it) and the whole team gets +1 flask and stronger flasks. All six give 8 flasks that heal 65% each.

Downed during a boss: teammates can revive you; each down makes the next revive slower (up to the third). Beating a Gatekeeper or Dungeon Master revives everyone on the spot. If the whole team is down, the expedition fails.

Controls: LMB attack · RMB secondary (melee) attack · Space movement skill · Q character skill · E ultimate (unlocks at level 10) · R drink a flask · T active item · F interact / revive · Shift sprint · G stickers.

Sprint drains stamina (the bar under your feet). Run it dry and you must wait until it is completely full again. Most Krystalkers cannot attack while sprinting; Crow, Rabbit, Serpent and Owl can.

Krystal Shrines restore you to full health while no monsters are nearby. If you die while exploring, you return at the nearest shrine that the Wildfire will not reach.

Ping with the middle mouse button. Pinging an enemy calls your bots to attack it.

Gems: win a run to find 5 random gems. Each Krystalker also has 3 legendary gems unlocked by personal quests.
