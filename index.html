How to play

Gather the Krystals

  1. The Hub. Pick your Krystalker and up to 3 gems at the Round Table, read the Archive, train on dummies, and set out through the Expedition Gate.
  2. Rooms have tiers. Easy rooms sit near the start; Normal, Hard and Deadly ones lie farther out. Each shows a recommended level when you enter. Skip a room now and come back later, even in Act II.
  3. Room bosses (☠, coloured by element) drop relics that favour that element, and raise a Krystal Shrine where you spend gold on levels. A boss you killed stays dead in Act II; its minions return.
  4. Gold flies straight to your purse and is shared by the whole team. Ambush rooms (padlock) lock until every monster is dead.
  5. The Wildfire ignites twice per Act: it closes once, holds, then closes again on a Gatekeeper. Act II repeats the map with stronger monsters. Then the Arena and your Dungeon Master.
  6. The Castle. Every map hides a triangular castle: three towers with Gatekeepers and a Keep with a Dungeon Master. It is optional and very hard; find it early, raid it late.

Elements and weaknesses: Fire, Frost, Bleed, Poison, Shock, Shadow. Bosses list their weakness: matching hits deal +50%, matching damage over time deals double. A frozen boss is freeze-immune for 12s.

Dying while exploring is not revivable: you respawn after a few seconds, drop one level, and leave that level's gold where you fell.

Downed during a boss: teammates can revive you; each down makes the next revive slower (up to the third). Beating a Gatekeeper or Dungeon Master revives everyone on the spot. If the whole team is down, the expedition fails.

Ping with the middle mouse button. Pinging an enemy calls your bots to attack it.

Gems: win a run to find 5 random gems. Each Krystalker also has 3 legendary gems unlocked by personal quests.
